## One remaining nuance (not a gap, needs a one-line note in compare.py)
A4 has 15 distinct hashes, not 14: the extra is (vs=1E6883FCCDE1F688,
ps=0000000000000000), appearing at A-positions 8 and 12 in BOTH runs.
Those are the only two mode-5 draws in the frame (rissue 50 and 56:
prim 8, idx 3, verts 3, depth+stencil on, EDRAM-height scissor 640x8192,
c0=0). Their A4 lines read ps=0 with pvalid=-1 (vvalid=1) — i.e. the pixel
shader is unbound at ConfigurePipeline, while routing-issue records the
guest ps hash A4A965C189287B99. That pattern is consistent with a
depth-only draw, but mode-5 semantics were not established in the report,
so treat it as plausible, not proven. Note also that compare.py's F4
invalid check only flags vvalid==0 or pvalid==0, so pvalid=-1 slips
through — worth a one-line fix in compare.py, not a backend change.
